Frequently Asked Questions

Answers to common questions about how AutoSpritze runs WhatsApp-based front-desk workflows.

How fast can we go live?

Most setups are ready within a few days after we configure your business details, hours, and booking flow rules.

Can we control what the AI says?

Yes. Responses are grounded with your knowledge base and can include strict fallback logic for sensitive questions.

Does it support booking workflows?

Yes. The system supports slot handling and admin-side booking management through the dashboard.

Can we still reply manually?

Absolutely. Your team can always step in and manage conversations directly when needed.

Is message history stored?

Yes, inbound and outbound records are stored for traceability and service quality review.

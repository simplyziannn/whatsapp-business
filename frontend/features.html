Product Features

Everything in AutoSpritze is built to automate front-desk operations over WhatsApp without losing control of quality.

Instant WhatsApp Replies

Responds immediately to repetitive customer enquiries so customers don’t wait and your team can focus on high-value conversations.

RAG Knowledge Answers

Uses your knowledge base content to keep responses grounded and reduce hallucinated answers in business-critical chats.

Booking Request Flow

Guides users into structured booking requests with clear status updates that admins can approve, reject, or cancel.

Admin Dashboard

View conversation history, booking data, and inbound-outbound message patterns from one operational interface.

Reliability + Dedup

Webhook idempotency and dedup layers prevent duplicate message handling during retries from external providers.

Rate-Limit Controls

Enforce daily usage limits and business rules so service quality remains stable even during high-volume periods.
